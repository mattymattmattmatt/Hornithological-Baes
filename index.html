Scoring rules

How points are calculated.

Each post earns:
points = (average rating out of 10) × (rarity multiplier) × (repeat penalty)
  • Super rare multiplier: 25
  • Rare multiplier: 15
  • Uncommon multiplier: 8
  • Common multiplier: 4
  • Very common multiplier: 1
  • If your post is a species already posted before → 50% points (repeat penalty = 0.5)
  • No ratings yet → that post contributes 0 points
  • Total points = sum of all your post points
